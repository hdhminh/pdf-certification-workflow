# FAQ

## Is This The Full Product Source Code?

No. This is a public blueprint repository only.

## Can Someone Deploy The Product From This Repository?

No. The private implementation, internal runtime assets, and deployment internals are intentionally excluded.

## Why Publish Example Contracts At All?

Because examples help reviewers understand the workflow boundary without exposing the real implementation.

## Why Avoid Naming Every Internal Technology Choice?

Because the point of this repository is to explain the product and its boundaries, not to disclose private engineering decisions unnecessarily.

## Who Is This Repository For?

- business stakeholders
- reviewers
- partners who need a product-level understanding
- anyone verifying ownership and public-facing project intent
